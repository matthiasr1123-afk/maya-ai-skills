## Learning loop: operating contract

1. **Observe:** inspect an actual generated video and the exact product reference.
2. **Decide:** POST / REGENERATE / DITCH with one main reason and one next action.
3. **Persist:** append evidence-based dated entry to [learning log](gemini-generation-learning-log.md) through GitHub when tools are available; confirm successful commit.
4. **Reuse:** read log before writing the next product-specific Gemini prompt; avoid repeated known failures.
5. **Measure:** connect published post metrics by actual video URL/ID when available. Track views, average watch time, completion, clicks, and sales as distinct fields. Never match by guesses.
6. **Improve:** prioritize formats with observed retention/view performance and accurate product representation.
7. **Audit:** do not mark a system synced without successful read + write + subsequent verification.

## Known pitfalls

- Exact stripes, bottle microtext, and buttons can be unreliable.
- Camera height, tilt, distance, crop and product frame occupancy must match reference.
- Coordinate outfits with vibrant, believable colors and use simple natural motion.
- Never call a generated video POST without inspecting it.
- Avoid generated text, watermarks, UI and unsupported claims.
