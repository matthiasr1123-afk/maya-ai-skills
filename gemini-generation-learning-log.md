# Maya Finds — Gemini Generation Learning Log

Purpose: Keep durable, evidence-based lessons from reviewed Gemini UGC generations. Primary KPI is views/retention; product clicks and sales are secondary. Never claim a video was reviewed unless the actual output was inspected.

## Review procedure
1. Compare generated video with exact product reference and viral reference (if supplied).
2. Apply `ugc-quality-control.md` and `shop-compliance.md`.
3. Assign **POST**, **REGENERATE**, or **DITCH**. Identify ONE biggest issue and ONE highest-impact next action.
4. After each reviewed generation, append a dated record below. Only write observed facts; mark uncertain observations as provisional. Never store personal identifiers, copyrighted source footage, or unsupported claims.
5. Before generating a new prompt, consult this log plus `gemini-prompt-engineer.md` and `fashion-director.md`. Avoid repeating known failure modes.
6. When an edit is requested, preserve past entries. Track results of retries and avoid repeatedly spending credits on the same failure.

## Established historical lessons (not newly inspected)
- Exact stripes and small labels have repeatedly been unreliable; do not keep regenerating intricate designs with unchanged instructions.
- Mask power-button inaccuracies and perfume bottle microtext discrepancies have raised compliance concerns.
- Lavender flare leggings: camera geometry and outfit color coordination were identified as important weaknesses.
- Favor reference-matched camera height, tilt, crop and product occupancy, vibrant coordinated outfits, one simple motion, realistic iPhone look, and 7–10 second fashion clips.
- Do not label historical examples POST/DITCH without a specific inspected result.
