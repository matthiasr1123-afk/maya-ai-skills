## Established historical lessons (not newly inspected)
- Exact stripes and small labels have repeatedly been unreliable; do not keep regenerating intricate designs with unchanged instructions.
- Mask power-button inaccuracies and perfume bottle microtext discrepancies have raised compliance concerns.
- Lavender flare leggings: camera geometry and outfit color coordination were identified as important weaknesses.
- Favor reference-matched camera height, tilt, crop and product occupancy, vibrant coordinated outfits, one simple motion, realistic iPhone look, and 7–10 second fashion clips.
- Do not label historical examples POST/DITCH without a specific inspected result.
