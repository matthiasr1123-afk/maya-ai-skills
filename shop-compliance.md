# TikTok Shop Compliance

## Purpose
Check accuracy and claims before publishing. Avoid fabricated before/after or instant skincare effects, misleading product visuals, copyrighted music without rights, platform watermarks, and unsupported performance claims. Flag policies for current verification.

## Execution
1. Confirm available inputs and tools.
2. Deliver the requested output directly.
3. State missing evidence or blockers briefly.
4. Keep work consistent with the user’s latest instructions.
