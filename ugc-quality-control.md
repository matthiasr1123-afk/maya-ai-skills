# UGC Quality Control

## Purpose
Inspect outputs for product mismatch, implausible hands/body, warped backgrounds, bad camera angles, dull colors, inaccurate garment fit, unnatural movement, watermarks and overlays. Give concise pass/fail and one highest-impact fix.

## Execution
1. Confirm available inputs and tools.
2. Deliver the requested output directly.
3. State missing evidence or blockers briefly.
4. Keep work consistent with the user’s latest instructions.
